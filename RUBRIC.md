# IMCP Harsh Grading Rubric

Grading scale per category: **0–5** (matching official form anchors) plus **bonus/penalty adjustments**.
Total: 100 pts content + technique. This rubric is deliberately harsher than the official one — it simulates an "exemplary-or-nothing" judge.

## Content (65 pts)

| # | Category | Pts | Exemplary (5) requires | Harsh deductions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Industry/Brand Knowledge | 5 | Specific facts: revenue, doors, founding story, SKUs, certifications — proves real research | -2 generic brand description; -3 factual errors |
| 2 | Business Opportunity | 5 | One crisp problem + measurable goal + why now | -2 vague objective ("grow awareness"); -3 no link to 45-day window |
| 3 | Research, Insights, Connection | 15 | 3+ cited stats/trends, each explicitly tied to a campaign decision ("X stat → therefore Y tactic") | -3 stats without sources; -5 research not connected to strategy; -2 outdated data |
| 4 | Campaign Strategy | 15 | Single big idea with a name, clear positioning, feasible for a real agency, creative differentiation | -4 generic "social media campaign"; -3 no point of view; -2 strategy doesn't solve stated problem |
| 5 | Activities & Schedule | 15 | Week-by-week 45-day timeline, channel-specific, realistic budgets implied, each activity maps to objective | -4 missing timeline; -3 channels not justified by research; -2 no creative samples described |
| 6 | Budget | 10 | Dollar amounts per line item, allocation justified by insight, ROI/financial viability addressed | -4 percentage-only budget; -3 no justification; -2 unrealistic numbers |
| 7 | Impact & Measurement | 10 | 3–5 KPIs with targets + tracking method per objective | -3 vanity metrics only; -2 no targets |

## Technique (35 pts — graded on plan quality as proxy for presentation)

| # | Category | Pts | Exemplary requires |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | Organization | 5 | Follows required outline exactly; logical flow problem→solution |
| 9 | Effectiveness | 5 | Campaign idea is memorable, ownable, pitch-worthy in one sentence |
| 9b | Differentiation | — | **Harsh add-on:** does the idea survive "could any brand do this?" test? Generic ideas capped at 3/5 |
| 10 | Delivery (proxy: writing clarity) | 5 | Tight, confident, no fluff; every sentence earns its place |
| 11 | Design readiness | 5 | Clear visual concept described; mockable assets identified |
| 12 | Overall Impression | 5 | Reads like a real agency pitch, not a school project |

## Automatic penalties (harsh mode)
- Campaign >45 days: **-10**
- No bibliography/unsourced claims: **-5**
- No creative samples: **-5**
- Objective not measurable: **-5**
- Brand already does this exact thing (no new angle): **-8**

## Scoring bands
- **90–100:** Lock it in — exemplify-ready
- **80–89:** Strong finalist
- **70–79:** Viable but needs work
- **<70:** Pass
