# Campaign Plan Template — IMCP Required Outline

Every generated plan MUST follow this structure (mirrors official deck outline):

## I. OVERVIEW (1 slide equivalent)
- Campaign name (ownable, memorable)
- The business problem in one sentence
- The campaign idea in one sentence
- The measurable objective

## II. PRODUCT & COMPANY DESCRIPTION
- Hero SKU + price + key benefits/certifications
- Company facts: founding, traction, distribution (with numbers)

## III. CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVES
- 2–3 SMART objectives tied to 45-day window (awareness → trial → sales)

## IV. TARGET MARKET
- Primary demo: age, psychographics, behaviors, media habits
- Secondary demo
- Market size estimate

## V. RESEARCH, INSIGHTS, CONNECTION
- 3–5 cited stats/trends (category: cultural, social, economic, tech, media consumption)
- Each insight ends with "→ therefore we will..."

## VI. CAMPAIGN STRATEGY, ACTIVITIES & SCHEDULE
- Positioning statement
- Big idea explanation (2–3 sentences)
- 45-day week-by-week timeline with channels
- Creative samples: describe 2–3 mockups (TikTok script concept, IG post, in-store POS, email, OOH)

## VII. PROPOSED BUDGET
- Table: category, $ amount, % of budget, justification tied to insight
- Total + projected ROI/financial viability note

## VIII. IMPACT & MEASUREMENT
- KPI table: metric, target, tracking method, tied to objective

## IX. BIBLIOGRAPHY
- All sources
