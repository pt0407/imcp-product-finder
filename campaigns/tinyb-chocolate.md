# IMCP Campaign Plan — tinyB Chocolate

**Case product:** tinyB chocolate — handmade Brazilian brigadeiro bars (gluten-free, small-batch)
**Campaign:** "The Candy That Crossed an Ocean"
**Duration:** 45 days | **Team role:** Account managers pitching client

## I. OVERVIEW

**Business problem:** tinyB makes the most story-rich chocolate in America — a 100-year-old Brazilian family recipe recreated by founder Renata after her mother's survival kitchen — but has near-zero brand awareness (~15K monthly site visits), so trial is limited to people who stumble onto it.

**Campaign idea:** "The Candy That Crossed an Ocean" — a sampling-first, story-led campaign that introduces brigadeiro to American snackers through the immigrant-family narrative that created it.

**Objective:** Drive trial among 18–34 food-curious consumers and convert story engagement into first purchases within 45 days.

## II. PRODUCT & COMPANY DESCRIPTION

- **Hero SKU:** Brigadeiro bars — handmade in small batches, gluten-free, less sugar than traditional brigadeiro, premium chocolate
- **Price:** ~$24–32/box (artisan pricing)
- **Story assets:** Founded by Renata (former nurse) in San Mateo, CA; recipe from her mother's kitchen in Brazil; "outrageous taste with a Brazilian soul"
- **Company facts:** Bootstrapped kitchen startup; products handmade in small batches; sold via DTC (tinybchocolate.com) and select local retail
- **Certifications/claims:** Gluten-free, handmade, real ingredients

## III. CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVES

1. **Awareness:** Reach 5M impressions among 18–34 foodies via TikTok/Instagram creator seeding
2. **Trial:** Drive 25,000 product samples distributed + 50,000 site visits in 45 days
3. **Sales:** Generate $150,000 in DTC revenue and 2,500 first-time orders

## IV. TARGET MARKET

**Primary:** "Curious Foodies" — 22–34, urban, TikTok/Instagram food content consumers, $60K+ income, already buy premium snacks ($8+ chocolate, fancy grocery). They share food finds and respond to founder stories.

**Secondary:** Brazilian/Latino diaspora + food-gift buyers (holiday/hostess gifting); Bay Area + NYC foodies for launch sampling.

**Market size:** ~22M US adults 22–34 who purchase premium/snack foods online monthly (IRI/ Numerator snacking data).

## V. RESEARCH, INSIGHTS, CONNECTION

1. **Immigrant/founder stories drive snack discovery:** Gen Z discovers products through people, not ads — 62% of Gen Z say creator recommendations matter more than brand messaging (Morning Consult). → Therefore the campaign centers Renata's real story, not product features.
2. **Food TikTok is the #1 discovery channel for snacks:** #FoodTok generates billions of views; taste-test and "story behind the food" formats consistently overperform (TikTok Creative Center food category benchmarks). → Therefore 70% of budget goes to creator seeding + Spark Ads on organic content.
3. **Sampling converts:** Brands report 3–5x higher trial-to-purchase rates from physical sampling vs. pure digital (Path to Purchase Institute). → Therefore 25K samples go to college campuses, food halls, and creator homes in launch cities.
4. **Premium chocolate is gifting-friendly:** Artisan chocolate gifting peaks around Valentine's/Mother's Day; small-batch + story = gift-worthy (National Confectioners Association seasonal data). → Therefore campaign includes a "Gifts With a Story" gifting component.
5. **Economic factor:** Inflation makes consumers selective about $25+ chocolate — perceived value must be justified. → Therefore all creative emphasizes handmade craft + real ingredients vs. mass-produced candy.

## VI. CAMPAIGN STRATEGY, ACTIVITIES & SCHEDULE

**Positioning:** "The chocolate bar with a soul" — not a snack, a story you can taste.

**Big idea:** Renata's story IS the media plan. Every touchpoint (creator videos, packaging inserts, emails) leads with the human story — her mother's kitchen, the recipe that survived an ocean — then the product.

**45-day timeline:**

| Weeks | Activity | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Creator seeding: 50 micro food creators receive product + story brief | TikTok/IG |
| 1–2 | "Brigadeiro 101" educational content series (what is brigadeiro, why it matters) | TikTok/IG/YT Shorts |
| 2–3 | Sampling events: 10 campus/food-hall pop-ups in NYC + SF | In-person |
| 3–4 | UGC push: #CandyThatCrossedAnOcean hashtag challenge — creators share their tasting + story reaction | TikTok/IG |
| 3–4 | Spark Ads amplify top organic creator content | Paid social |
| 4–5 | Gifting push: "Gifts With a Story" landing page + email campaign to existing customers | Email/DTC |
| 4–5 | PR push: founder interview tour (food podcasts, local TV) | Earned media |

**Creative samples:**
- **TikTok concept:** 30-sec founder video — Renata in her kitchen showing the recipe, "My mom made this to survive. I made it to share." (Storyboard: kitchen → ingredient close-ups → first bite reaction)
- **IG carousel:** "5 things you don't know about brigadeiro" — history, cultural significance, tasting notes
- **In-store/packaging insert:** Story card — "This recipe crossed an ocean" with founder photo + QR to story video
- **Email:** "Meet the woman behind your chocolate" — founder letter + product
