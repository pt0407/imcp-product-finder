# IMCP Campaign Plan — Candycopia

**Case product:** Candycopia — specialty candy store (Oak Park + Elmhurst, IL) + DTC e-commerce
**Campaign:** "Build Your Own Candy Bar"
**Duration:** 45 days | **Team role:** Account managers pitching client

## I. OVERVIEW

**Business problem:** Candycopia has the most engaging product experience in candy — customers design custom gummy mixes from 200+ options at in-store bars — but that experience is trapped inside two Chicago suburbs. Online, it's a confusing catalog with no hook.

**Campaign idea:** "Build Your Own Candy Bar" — turn the in-store experience into a shareable UGC engine: customers design their mix, post it, and enter to win; the best mixes become limited-run products.

**Objective:** Convert the experiential advantage into national awareness + e-commerce subscription sales within 45 days.

## II. PRODUCT & COMPANY DESCRIPTION

- **Hero experience:** Custom candy bar — pick from 200+ gummies, international treats, nostalgic favorites, small-batch makers
- **Price:** ~$15–30/bag or custom mix; subscription option
- **Story assets:** Women-owned, family-run (the Mason family); partners with local women/diverse-owned candymakers; "every bite tastes good, feels good, does good"
- **Company facts:** Founded 2022; 2 storefronts (Oak Park, Elmhurst IL); ~8 employees; 297K monthly site visits; ships nationwide
- **Differentiator:** Only "Swedish Pick N Mix" bar in the Midwest; discovery-focused assortment

## III. CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVES

1. **Awareness:** Generate 10M impressions + 1M engagements via UGC challenge
2. **Trial:** Drive 15,000 custom-mix orders or sample kit redemptions
3. **Sales:** Launch "Community Mixes" subscription; acquire 1,000 subscribers in 45 days

## IV. TARGET MARKET

**Primary:** "Candy Curious" — 18–34, TikTok/IG users who engage with food challenges, customization, and nostalgic treats. Urban/suburban, $50K+ income, gift-givers.

**Secondary:** Candy store visitors/tourists in Chicago; parents buying kids' treat experiences; candy subscription gifters.

**Market size:** ~$45B US candy market; customization/experiential segment growing ~8% YoY.

## V. RESEARCH, INSIGHTS, CONNECTION

1. **Customization drives candy engagement:** Gen Z treats food as self-expression; custom candy/bar concepts consistently trend on TikTok (#CandyBars, #GummyMix billions of views). → Therefore the campaign makes the customer the product developer.
2. **UGC challenges outperform brand content:** TikTok hashtag challenges generate 5–10x higher engagement than brand-posted content (TikTok marketing benchmarks). → Therefore we seed creators but let customers create the content.
3. **Experiential retail converts to DTC:** Brands like Erewhon and local candy shops report social-driven e-commerce spikes after in-store experiences go viral. → Therefore we bridge the physical "candy bar" experience to online ordering via the mix builder.
4. **Subscription candy is a proven model:** Candy subscriptions (e.g., Candy Club, SnackCrate) show high retention; novelty/customization reduces churn. → Therefore "Community Mixes" become a subscription product.
5. **Economic factor:** Candy is an affordable indulgence; $15–30 custom bags fit "little treat" budgets even in tight economies. → Therefore pricing stays accessible.

## VI. CAMPAIGN STRATEGY, ACTIVITIES & SCHEDULE

**Positioning:** "The candy store that lets you design your own fix" — candy as self-expression, not just sugar.

**Big idea:** "Build Your Own Candy Bar" UGC challenge — customers create their dream candy mix (in-store or via a simple online builder), post a photo/video, tag @candycopia. Weekly winners' mixes get produced as limited-run "Community Mixes" — turning customers into product developers.

**45-day timeline:**

| Weeks | Activity | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Launch challenge + online mix builder tool (simple web app) | Website/social |
| 1–2 | Seed creators: 30 food/lifestyle creators visit stores, build mixes, post | TikTok/IG |
| 2–3 | Challenge push: hashtag #BuildYourOwnCandyBar, weekly prizes | TikTok/IG |
| 3 | First "Community Mix" drop — best mix produced and sold | E-commerce |
| 3–4 | Paid amplification of top UGC | Meta/TikTok Spark Ads |
| 4–5 | Subscription push: "Never Run Out of Your Mix" email + social | Email/social |
| 4–5 | PR: local news + food media coverage of challenge winners | Earned media |

**Creative samples:**
- **TikTok concept:** 20-sec "I built my own candy bar and it slaps" — POV mixing gummies, ASMR pour sounds, taste reaction
- **IG carousel:** "This week's Community Mix" — photo of the winning mix + creator story
- **In-store POS:** Counter display — "Your mix could be next" QR to challenge
- **Email:** "You voted. We made it. It's live." — Community Mix launch
