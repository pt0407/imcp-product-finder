# IMCP Campaign Plan — Boba Tea Protein

**Case product:** Boba Tea Protein — functional bubble tea protein drink
**Campaign:** "Bubble Tea That Works"
**Duration:** 45 days | **Team role:** Account managers pitching client

## I. OVERVIEW

**Business problem:** Boba Tea Protein makes the most Gen-Z-native functional drink imaginable — real bubble tea taste + protein — but is unknown outside a small follower base. The product is inherently viral; the brand just hasn't activated it.

**Campaign idea:** "Bubble Tea That Works" — position protein bubble tea as the afternoon pick-me-up that actually fuels you, using TikTok-native content and sampling to drive trial.

**Objective:** Build awareness + drive trial/purchase among 18–34 Gen Z within 45 days.

## II. PRODUCT & COMPANY DESCRIPTION

- **Hero SKU:** Protein bubble tea — real tea + protein, boba pearls, functional benefits
- **Price:** ~$5–8/unit or multipack
- **Story assets:** Functional beverage for the boba generation; protein + familiar format
- **Company facts:** 2–10 employees; 133K monthly site visits; DTC + select retail; products include multiple flavors/varieties
- **Certifications/claims:** Protein content, clean-ish ingredients, functional positioning

## III. CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVES

1. **Awareness:** Reach 10M impressions among Gen Z via TikTok-first content
2. **Trial:** Drive 30,000 samples + 50,000 site visits
3. **Sales:** Generate 5,000 first-time orders + $75K revenue

## IV. TARGET MARKET

**Primary:** "Gen Z Optimizers" — 18–29, TikTok-native, gym/fitness-adjacent, buy protein products but hate protein shakes' taste. $40K+ income/student, impulse buyers.

**Secondary:** Boba tea regulars seeking functional upgrade; college students; busy professionals wanting afternoon fuel.

**Market size:** Functional beverages market ~$15B+; boba tea US market ~$3B+ and growing; protein drink market ~$20B.

## V. RESEARCH, INSIGHTS, CONNECTION

1. **Gen Z wants functional but delicious:** 73% of Gen Z seek functional benefits in food/drink, but taste is the #1 purchase driver — they reject "healthy but tastes bad" (IFIC/FMCG trend reports). → Therefore we never say "protein drink" — we say "bubble tea that actually works."
2. **TikTok is the launchpad for food virality:** Food/beverage TikTok content has the highest share rate of any category; taste-test and "can't believe it's healthy" formats dominate. → Therefore 70% of budget goes to TikTok creator seeding + Spark Ads.
3. **Sampling drives beverage trial:** Drink sampling (gyms, campuses, events) converts at high rates; taste is the barrier for unfamiliar functional drinks. → Therefore 30K samples go to college campuses, gyms, and boba shops.
4. **Protein demand is rising:** GLP-1 era + fitness culture = protein-seeking behavior at all-dayparts, not just post-workout. → Therefore we position for the 2 p.m. crash, not just gym.
5. **Economic factor:** $5–8 is impulse-friendly; protein bubble tea replaces $6 coffee + snack. → Therefore value framing: "your afternoon fuel."

## VI. CAMPAIGN STRATEGY, ACTIVITIES & SCHEDULE

**Positioning:** "Bubble tea that actually works" — the functional drink that doesn't taste like a supplement.

**Big idea:** "Bubble Tea That Works" — every piece of content is a taste-test: creators try it expecting "healthy drink = bad," then react to how good it actually tastes. The surprise IS the hook.

**45-day timeline:**

| Weeks | Activity | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Creator seeding: 60 fitness/food/lifestyle micro creators receive product | TikTok/IG |
| 1–2 | "I tried protein bubble tea" taste-test content series | TikTok/IG |
| 2–3 | Sampling tour: 15 college campuses + gyms + boba shops | In-person |
| 3–4 | UGC push: #BubbleTeaThatWorks — creators share reactions | TikTok/IG |
| 3–4 | Paid amplification of top organic content | TikTok Spark/Meta |
| 4–5 | "2 p.m. fuel" positioning content — afternoon crash angle | TikTok/IG |
| 4–5 | PR: fitness/food media outreach | Earned media |

**Creative samples:**
- **TikTok concept:** 20-sec "I was NOT expecting this to taste good" — creator opens protein bubble tea, first sip reaction, checks nutrition label
- **IG carousel:** "Protein bubble tea vs. your afternoon crash" — problem/solution
- **In-store POS:** "Tastes like boba. Works like protein." counter card
- **Email:** "Your 2 p.m. just got better" — product + subscription pitch
