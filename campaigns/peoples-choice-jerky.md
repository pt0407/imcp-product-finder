# IMCP Campaign Plan — People's Choice Beef Jerky

**Case product:** People's Choice Beef Jerky — handcrafted beef jerky, family recipe since 1929, made in Los Angeles
**Campaign:** "Since 1929"
**Duration:** 45 days | **Team role:** Account managers pitching client

## I. OVERVIEW

**Business problem:** People's Choice makes the oldest continuously-operated jerky recipe in America — four generations, 96 years, handmade in LA — but competes in a $1.5B category dominated by mass-market brands (Jack Link's, Slim Jim) with zero awareness of its heritage.

**Campaign idea:** "Since 1929" — position the brand as "the original American jerky," using the family story and big-slab format to differentiate from processed meat sticks.

**Objective:** Build brand awareness + trial among jerky/snack consumers within 45 days.

## II. PRODUCT & COMPANY DESCRIPTION

- **Hero SKU:** Big Slab Classic Beef Jerky — whole-muscle beef, hand-sliced, never chopped & formed, 14g protein
- **Price:** ~$7/bag (2.5oz), ~$32/case
- **Story assets:** Founded 1929 by John Bianchetti as a butcher shop; 4th-generation Brian Bianchetti now runs it; recipe unchanged for 90+ years; "LA's original beef jerky"
- **Company facts:** Family-owned, LA-based; 214K monthly site visits; 2–10 employees; DTC + select retail
- **Formats:** Big slabs, old-fashioned dry jerky, tasting kitchen flavors, beef sticks
- **Certifications/claims:** 100% USA beef, keto-approved, no fillers, handmade small-batch

## III. CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVES

1. **Awareness:** Reach 6M impressions among jerky/meat-snack buyers
2. **Trial:** Drive 20,000 samples + 30,000 site visits
3. **Sales:** Generate $200K revenue + 3,000 first-time orders

## IV. TARGET MARKET

**Primary:** "Meat Snackers" — 18–45, male-skewing but increasingly mixed, fitness/hunting/camping/outdoor adjacent, buy jerky at gas stations/grocery. $50K+ income.

**Secondary:** LA/regional foodies; keto/carnivore diet community; gift buyers (jerky gift boxes exist).

**Market size:** ~$1.5B US jerky market; premium/natural segment growing ~7% YoY.

## V. RESEARCH, INSIGHTS, CONNECTION

1. **Heritage/craft is the anti-mass-market differentiator:** Consumers increasingly choose "real food" over processed; jerky buyers specifically seek "real beef, no fillers" (FMI protein snack trends). → Therefore we lead with 96-year recipe + whole-muscle slabs vs. processed sticks.
2. **LA food culture is aspirational:** LA has the highest restaurant/foodie concentration in the US; "LA's original" carries cultural weight. → Therefore we lean into LA origin + local pride.
3. **Sampling drives jerky trial:** Jerky is a taste-driven repurchase category; in-person sampling at gyms/outdoor stores converts. → Therefore 20K samples go to gyms, outdoor retailers, and LA food events.
4. **Story content works for food:** Founder/family story content (like Mid-Day Squares' founder-led approach) builds loyal audiences for food brands. → Therefore Brian Bianchetti's family story is the content engine.
5. **Economic factor:** $7/bag is impulse-friendly; jerky is a pantry staple during economic uncertainty (protein/snack trading down from restaurants). → Therefore emphasize value + protein content.

## VI. CAMPAIGN STRATEGY, ACTIVITIES & SCHEDULE

**Positioning:** "The original American jerky" — not a snack, a 96-year tradition.

**Big idea:** "Since 1929" — every touchpoint tells the family story: the butcher shop origin, the unchanged recipe, the big-slab format that mass brands can't replicate. Content shows the actual hands making it.
