# IMCP Campaign Plan — Snack Hawaii

**Case product:** Snack Hawaii — authentic Hawaiian & Asian snacks DTC (Li Hing Mui, Ahi jerky, crack seed, macadamia, coffee)
**Campaign:** "Aloha in a Box"
**Duration:** 45 days | **Team role:** Account managers pitching client

## I. OVERVIEW

**Business problem:** Snack Hawaii invented products people can't get anywhere else — Ahi Jerky, Li Hing Mui — but the brand is invisible outside Hawaii. 230K monthly site visits prove demand exists; the brand just doesn't reach the mainland snackers who'd love it.

**Campaign idea:** "Aloha in a Box" — mystery Hawaiian snack boxes + creator "taste of Hawaii" content that turns the islands' most addictive snacks into a shareable subscription.

**Objective:** Convert mainland curiosity into subscription sales within 45 days.

## II. PRODUCT & COMPANY DESCRIPTION

- **Hero SKU:** "Aloha Box" — monthly mystery box of Hawaiian snacks (already exists, underpromoted)
- **Price:** ~$40–60/box
- **Story assets:** Family-owned (Asia Trans & Co., Kona HI); invented Ahi Jerky and Marlin Jerky; 25+ years; products in Walmart/Costco Hawaii
- **Company facts:** 6 employees; 230K monthly visits; ships nationwide + internationally; LinkedIn has 88 followers = near-zero B2B/brand presence
- **Products:** Li Hing Mui, crack seed, dried seafood, macadamia, Kona coffee, candies — 465 SKUs

## III. CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVES

1. **Awareness:** Reach 8M impressions among food-curious mainland consumers
2. **Trial:** Drive 10,000 Aloha Box first-time purchases
3. **Sales:** Convert 30% of first-time buyers to subscription; generate $400K revenue

## IV. TARGET MARKET

**Primary:** "Taste Adventurers" — 22–40, foodie/TikTok snack explorers, travelers, people who've visited Hawaii or dream of it. $60K+ income, gift-givers.

**Secondary:** Hawaiian diaspora (mainland Hawaiian communities); travelers buying gifts at airports; parents seeking unique kids' snacks.

**Market size:** Hawaiian food/tourism is a $17B+ industry; snack subscription market growing ~10% YoY.

## V. RESEARCH, INSIGHTS, CONNECTION

1. **Mystery boxes are proven DTC:** Mystery/snack subscription boxes (e.g., SnackCrate, Bokksu) show high social virality and repeat purchase rates. → Therefore we lead with the Aloha Box mystery format, not individual SKUs.
2. **Hawaii content overperforms:** #Hawaii food/travel content is consistently top-tier on TikTok/IG; "things you can only get in Hawaii" is a proven viral format. → Therefore creators frame products as "the snack you can't get anywhere else."
3. **Travelers buy regional foods:** Airport/travel retail and post-trip snack purchases are established behavior; Hawaii visitors spend $30B+/year. → Therefore we target travelers + diaspora, not just foodies.
4. **Story > product for niche foods:** Authentic regional foods succeed when the story is as good as the taste (e.g., Vietnamese coffee boom). → Therefore we emphasize "invented in Kona" + family heritage.
5. **Economic factor:** $40–60 box is an affordable splurge/gift; mystery format lowers purchase friction vs. choosing unfamiliar snacks. → Therefore box-first, not SKU-first.

## VI. CAMPAIGN STRATEGY, ACTIVITIES & SCHEDULE

**Positioning:** "The taste of Hawaii, delivered" — not a snack brand, a vacation in a box.

**Big idea:** "Aloha in a Box" — each monthly box is a mystery tasting journey through Hawaii's flavors, paired with a story card explaining each snack's origin (Li Hing Mui history, how Ahi Jerky was invented, Kona coffee culture). Creators film "unboxing + first bite" reactions; the strangeness IS the content.

**45-day timeline:**

| Weeks | Activity | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Launch Aloha Box subscription push + creator seeding (40 food/travel creators) | TikTok/IG |
| 1–2 | "Only in Hawaii" content series — creators taste-test unfamiliar snacks | TikTok/IG/YT |
| 2–3 | Airport/travel retail pitch + sampling at LAX/SFO/HNL gift shops | In-person/PR |
| 3 | Paid amplification of top reaction content | Meta/TikTok Spark |
| 3–4 | "Send Aloha" gifting push — Mother's Day/Father's Day angle | Email/social |
| 4–5 | Subscriber retention content: "This month's island story" | Email/social |
| 4–5 | PR: food media + travel press outreach | Earned media |

**Creative samples:**
- **TikTok concept:** "I tried the weirdest snack from Hawaii" — creator opens Aloha Box, tastes Li Hing Mui/Ahi Jerky, genuine reaction arc
- **IG carousel:** "6 snacks you can ONLY get in Hawaii" — product photos + stories
- **Packaging insert:** "Your snack's island story" — QR to founder/family video
- **Email:** "This box tastes like a vacation" — subscription pitch + past box photos
