# Judge Intel — scraped from official DECA sources (2026-11-07)

## FORMAT CHANGE (2026-27)
IMCP no longer has a 10-page written entry. Deliverable = 20-slide pitch deck
(cover, title, TOC, body, bibliography, appendix ALL count toward 20).
The live 15-min presentation (incl. Q&A) IS the judged score — presentation
weighted 2x the cluster exam. New standardized technique section:
organization, persuasion, delivery, design, overall impression.
Sources: deca.org/compete/imcp, arkansasdeca toolkit, decadirect pitch-deck FAQ.

## HARD RULES (verbatim from official guidelines)
- NO FOOD OR DRINKS allowed. <- kills taste-test props (Stryve demo banned)
- Items of monetary value may be HANDED to judges but NOT LEFT with them.
  <- Woobles kit/cube can be passed around, must come back. Can't gift.
- Leave-behinds allowed: flyers, brochures, pamphlets, business cards ONLY.
  <- best leave-behind = branded card/flyer/coupon card.
- Visual aids: hand-carry only, participants carry + set up, no wheeled carts,
  setup time counts in the 15 min, NO electrical power or internet supplied,
  no generators, sound OK at conversational level, bring own charged devices.
- Statement of Assurances: physical/digital signature, no typed fonts — -15 pts.
- Over 20 slides: -5 each. Unnumbered slides: -5. Not 16:9/typed: -5.
- EVERY participant must present AND answer at least one judge question.

## WHAT REAL JUDGES SAY (decadirect judge interviews)
- First few seconds make/break it; greeting + handshake sets the outcome.
- Mistakes happen — composure is what's scored. Never unravel.
- Think like a decision-maker: costs, timelines, measurable benefits, real stats.
  "Judges can tell when something's too idealistic."
- Judge = your prospective client; don't insult the company's current strategy.
- Exec summary gets read fully; the rest gets skimmed.
- Don't just recite the deck — the presentation is the differentiator.
- Use exact rubric vocabulary. Have a plan B if a visual aid fails.
- Practice ≥2x/week; record and review.
- Texas district CDCs officially listed in January (e.g. Jan 12-14, 2027);
  a December district = same format, verify local penalty enforcement.

## STRATEGIC IMPLICATIONS
1. Stryve taste test = BANNED (no food). v17 deck still the rubric leader but
   the prop plan dies. Backup demo needed or accept visual-only pitch.
2. Props must be: pocketable, battery-free or charged, <$30 handed-back, or
   pure print leave-behinds.
3. Creative samples (10 pts) double as leave-behinds if printed as flyers/cards.
4. The whole score is the live pitch — delivery choreography (handoffs, equal
   airtime, everyone answers) matters as much as deck content at district.
5. Exam still carries real weight (presentation = 2x exam, exam persists).
