I. Overview

Campaign Overview

  • Thesis: Stryve does not need more doors - it needs shoppers who try it once to come back [O1].
  • Problem: net sales fell ~41% to $17.7M (FY2023) while distribution grew to H-E-B, Walmart, Whole Foods and more - a velocity problem, per its own 10-K [S1][R5].
  • Opportunity: 29% of 1,400 reviews praise taste unprompted and 20% reorder - the product converts once people try it [R1][R4].
  • Solution: 'It's Not Jerky' puts Braav biltong in the jerky shopper's decision at DFW H-E-Bs and online [O1].
  • 45-day goal: 2,000 attributed purchases and +25% units per store vs. control - the test still runs if the clip strip is declined [O1].

II. Product & Company

The Product

  • Braav biltong: South African air-dried beef - cured and air-dried, never cooked like jerky; thick, tender, intensely beefy [S10].
  • Clean label: sugar-free, gluten-free, keto/paleo/whole30-friendly, 30g+ protein per serving; Original, Steak Bites, and Zero Sugar Hickory lines [S10][S4].
  • Retail ~$8-9 per 2.25oz bag (Sprouts $7.99, Walmart multipacks); Plano, TX HQ [S4][S1].
  • Sold DTC, on Amazon, and in H-E-B, Walmart, Whole Foods, Sprouts, CVS, Circle K and Love's [S2][S3].

II. Product & Company

The Company & Its Position

  • Stryve Foods Corp (NASDAQ: SNAX), Plano TX; founded by Ted Casey (Dymatize), ex-NFL player Gabe Carimi, and South African native Warren Pala [S1][S10].

  • Position: premium air-dried beef for performance and clean-label shoppers - the jerky upgrade, not another jerky [S10][R2].

IV. Target Market

Target Market

  • P1 Performance Snackers, 18-34: DFW/Texas jerky buyers focused on macros; keto/paleo/whole30-curious; buy on protein and clean labels [S6][R2].
  • P2 Clean-Label H-E-B Parents, 30-50: weekly H-E-B shoppers wanting no-sugar, recognizable-ingredient snacks for lunchboxes [S6][S9].

V. Research, Insights & Connection

Research Method & Results

  • Secondary (complete): 10-K via last10k, company site/stores, Walmart/Sprouts listings, trade press - 10 sources [S1][S2][S3][S4][S5].
  • Primary, no-survey (complete): keyword-coded analysis of 1,400 public Stamped.io reviews [R1][R4] and a public distribution/digital-shelf audit of 8 sources [R5][R6] [S11][S12]; survey/interviews not run - published reviews skew positive.
  • Key results: sales -41% while doors multiplied [S1][R5]; taste and reorder dominate reviews [R1][R4]; only 4% discovered it in a store [R3]; biltong is an unfamiliar word in Texas [R6].
  • Influences: protein-snack boom (social), clean-label/keto trend (cultural), meat-snack growth +11.3% (market), retail app coupons (technological) [S6][S7][S9].

V. Research, Insights & Connection

Insights -> Campaign Decisions

  • Finding: 29% of 1,400 reviews praise taste unprompted; complaints only 4% [R1] -> demos and taste content lead, not awareness ads.
  • Finding: only 4% discovered it in a store; the review base is DTC/repeat buyers [R3] -> retail trial must be manufactured at the shelf.
  • Finding: sales -41% while distribution grew [S1][R5] -> fund trial at stores already carrying it, not new distribution.
  • Finding: 12% cite clean-label benefits; 5% compare it to jerky [R2] -> position biltong as the jerky upgrade for performance and clean-label shoppers.
  • Finding: 20% reorder or buy bulk [R4] -> end with a bundle/repeat offer; the product already earns reorders.

VI. Strategy, Activities & Schedule

Campaign Strategy

  • Big idea: It's Not Jerky - biltong is air-dried, never cooked; a different product for a different shopper. Message: 'Love jerky? Try biltong.'
  • Positioning: for performance and clean-label shoppers who want more from a meat snack, Braav is the air-dried beef upgrade [S10][R2].
  • IMC mix: Paid (geo social) + Earned (Plano founder story + ex-NFL credibility) + Shared (#ItsNotJerky taste-off duets) + Owned (email/SMS, shelf talkers).
  • Push-pull: H-E-B gets a measurable velocity test (push); shoppers get samples and a coupon (pull) [O1][O2].

VI. Strategy, Activities & Schedule

Program 1: Biltong 101 Demo Days

  • Biltong 101 in person: 12 weekend demo shifts at 6 DFW H-E-B test stores; 6 control stores get the coupon only [O1][O2].
  • Mechanic: 'What is biltong?' sample cups at the jerky/meat-snack set; QR to '$2 off 2 bags'; 300 samples per shift = 3,600 total [A7][A8].
  • Why: biltong is an unfamiliar word in Texas [R6] and only 4% of reviewers found it in a store [R3] - the shelf is where education must happen.
  • Measured: QR scans per store and units per store vs. control stores [O1].

VII. Proposed Budget

Proposed Budget & Financial Viability

  • Total $15,000: paid social $5,500 [A4]; creators 3 x $1,000 = $3,000 [A6]; demos 12 x $250 = $3,000 [A7]; production $900 [A9]; tools $400 [A10]; contingency $2,200 [A11].
  • Funnel: 1.5M impressions x 1.5% x 10% = 2,250 purchases; half H-E-B coupon (~$8 net), half online packs (~$28) = $18 avg, $40.5K [A1][A4].
  • The $2 coupon is manufacturer-funded, already netted in blended revenue - no separate line [A1][A8].
  • Gross profit at 45% = $18.2K; profit ROI 21%; ROAS 4.3x on $9.4K ad+creator spend [A2].
  • Conservative (50%): ~1,125 purchases, ROI ~-40%; 30% repeat in 90 days + retained retail doors recover it [A13]. Upside: ~720 demo-day purchases [A15].
