METHODS

KEY RESULTS
Content analysis of 1,400 public reviews (collected 2026-11-07): 29% mention taste/flavor unprompted, 20% mention repeat/bulk orders, complaints only 4%
12% of 1,400 reviews mention health/clean-label benefits (sugar-free, high protein, keto) and 5% explicitly compare it to jerky or other meat snacks
Only 4% of 1,400 reviews mention discovering Stryve in a store; the review base is overwhelmingly DTC/repeat buyers
20% of 1,400 reviews mention repeat purchase or bulk orders - the strongest loyalty signal in the dataset
