Program 1: Biltong 101 Demo Days
  • It's Not Jerky in person: 12 weekend Biltong 101 demo shifts at 6 DFW H-E-B test stores; 6 control stores get the coupon only.
  • Mechanic: 'What is biltong?' sample cups at the jerky/meat-snack set; QR to '$2 off 2 bags'; 300 samples per shift = 3,600 total.
  • Why: biltong is an unfamiliar word in Texas and only 4% of reviewers found it in a store - the shelf is where education must happen.
  • Measured: QR scans per store and units per store vs. control stores.
