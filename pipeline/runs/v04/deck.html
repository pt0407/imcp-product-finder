I. Overview

Campaign Overview

  • Thesis: Pan's does not need more stores - it needs more bags sold per store [S1].
  • Problem: Pan's grew from ~750 to 2,500+ stores, yet revenue fell from $3.15M (FY22) and the company loses money every year [S1][S2][S4].
  • Business opportunity: meat snacks grew 11.3% to $6.4B while plant-based meat fell 10% - Pan's is filed in the wrong mental aisle [S6][S7].
  • Solution: 'Switch to Mushroom Mode' moves Pan's into the jerky shopper's decision at 10 Central Texas H-E-B stores [O1].
  • 45-day goal: 6,000 attributed purchases and +25% units per store vs. control stores [O1].

II. Product & Company

The Product

  • Shiitake mushroom jerky from a decades-old Pan family recipe found in Malaysia; chewy, savory, umami [S2].
  • Vegan, gluten-free, Kosher, no allergens to declare; the brand says it 'isn't trying to completely imitate beef' [S5].
  • Shelf price $7.99-$8.99 per 2.2 oz bag; cost to make about $1.67 per bag at launch [S3].
  • Four core flavors; sold online, on Amazon, and in grocery including H-E-B in Texas [S2][S10].

IV. Target Market

Target Market

  • P1 Gen-Z Snack Explorers, 14-24: weekly snack buyers in Central Texas who try snacks from TikTok and friends; skeptical of fake meat [S8][S11].
  • P2 Flexitarian H-E-B Parents, 30-50: weekly H-E-B shoppers using app coupons; want snacks with no allergens to worry about [S5][S9].
  • Both share one need: a savory snack that is honest about what it is - the reason Pan's says it is not imitating beef [S5].
  • Market: 6 in 10 US households already buy plant-based foods [S11].

V. Research, Insights & Connection

Research Method & Results

  • Secondary (complete): SEC filing, GFI/SPINS, Circana via trade press, company pages - 11 sources [S1][S6][S7][S8].
  • Key results: revenue down while stores up [S1][S2]; meat snacks +11.3% [S7]; plant-based meat -10% [S6]; jerky flat while meat sticks surge [S8].
  • Primary (in field): 10-question survey of Central Texas snack buyers, 3-5 shopper interviews with taste test, and an H-E-B shelf audit at 3 stores.
  • Influences: protein snacking boom (social), plant-based fatigue (cultural), food inflation (economic), app coupons (technological) [S6][S7][S9].

V. Research, Insights & Connection

Insights -> Campaign Decisions

  • Finding: store count up, revenue down [S1][S2] -> therefore we fund trial at the shelf (Demo Days), not more distribution.
  • Finding: meat snacks +11.3%, plant-based meat -10% [S6][S7] -> therefore Pan's is placed and messaged next to jerky, not with fake meat (Aisle Switch).
  • Finding: brand says it is not imitating beef [S5] -> therefore creators run honest taste-offs instead of 'tastes just like beef' claims.
  • Finding: losses every year [S1] -> therefore every dollar is trackable (coupons, QR, test vs. control).

VI. Strategy, Activities & Schedule

Campaign Strategy

  • Big idea: Mushroom Mode - not fake meat, a savory snack you switch into. Message: 'Love jerky? Switch to Mushroom Mode.'
  • Positioning: for Texas snackers who want something savory and honest, Pan's is the jerky made from shiitake that does not pretend to be beef [S5].
  • IMC mix: Paid (geo social) + Earned (Shark Tank founder story) + Shared (#MushroomMode duets) + Owned (email/SMS, shelf talkers).
  • Push-pull: H-E-B gets a measurable velocity test (push); shoppers get samples and a coupon (pull) [O1][O2].

VI. Strategy, Activities & Schedule

Program 1: Mushroom Mode Demo Days

  • Mushroom Mode in person: 20 weekend demo shifts at 10 Central Texas H-E-B stores [O1][O2].
  • Mechanic: sample cups at the jerky set; QR code to 'buy 2 save $3'; target 200 samples per shift = 4,000 total [A7].
  • Why: shoppers do not buy what they cannot imagine tasting - sampling converts curiosity at the shelf.
  • Measured: QR scans per store and sales lift vs. matched control stores [O1].

VII. Proposed Budget

Proposed Budget & Financial Viability

  • Total $15,000: paid social $6,000 [A4]; creators 4 x $750 = $3,000 [A6]; demos 20 x $150 = $3,000 [A7]; coupon $1,500 [A8]; production $800 [A9]; tools $300 [A10]; contingency $400 [A11].
  • Funnel: 3.5M impressions x 1.5% CTR x 12% purchase = 6,300 purchases x $8 brand revenue = $50.4K [A1][A4].
  • Gross profit at 42% = $21.2K; profit ROI 41%; ROAS 5.6x on $9K ad spend [A2].
  • Break-even 4,464 purchases. Conservative case (50%) 3,150 loses $4.4K in-window - offset by repeat buyers and keeping shelf space [O1].
