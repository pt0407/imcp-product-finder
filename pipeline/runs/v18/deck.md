## Slide 4 — I. Overview: Campaign Overview
- Thesis: PUCKIPUPPY's product wins - its delivery journey loses buyers. 11% of 500 reviews are 1-star; ~68% of those cite shipping damage, assembly, app issues, or support [R1][S5].
- Big idea: Arrive Ready - every order ships with a damage-claim promise, QR setup concierge, and finance/trade-in path; trust badges move above the fold [R6].
- Demo: judges sit on the Labrador Pro and ride - the product demos itself [S2].
- Goal: 90 attributed orders + 400 test rides + cut damage-review share in 45 days.

*Visual (image):* Labrador Pro product shot

> Talk track: Open with the stat: 1 in 9 reviews is 1-star, and most blame the delivery, not the bike.

## Slide 5 — II. Product & Company: The Product
- PUCKIPUPPY makes dog-breed-named ebikes - Labrador Pro (~$1,399), Boxer ST, Corgi, Husky trike - for riders 28-50 who want car-free trips [S2].
- UL 2849/2271 certified; torque sensor praised as 'natural' feel; 80-mile range in reviews [S2][S5].
- Problem: $1,299-$1,699 sticker + freight shipping + self-assembly = the highest-anxiety purchase in the category [R1][R4].

*Visual (image):* Real PUCKIPUPPY Labrador Pro photography

> Talk track: Pass around the photo; 'this is a $1,400 decision made on a website - here's where it breaks.'

## Slide 6 — II. Product & Company: The Company & Its Position

- DTC-only vs. 'affordable for all' promise; no mass retail presence found in audit - whitespace is experience, not distribution [R4].

## Slide 9 — V. Research, Insights & Connection: Research Method & Results
- Secondary (complete): 8 sources - about/press, product pages, Trustpilot, Tom's Guide, third-party metrics, industry data [S1][S3][S4][S6][S8].
- Primary, no-survey (complete): keyword-coded analysis of 500 public Judge.me reviews incl. 1-star deep-dive [R1][R2]; digital shelf/price audit of 6 sources [R4][R6].
- Key results: product satisfies intact-delivery buyers [R2]; negatives cluster on damage+support ~68% of 1-stars [R1]; trust assets buried [R6]; repeat path thin [R3].
- Influences: e-bike category growth (market), review-driven purchase behavior (cultural), DTC fulfillment expectations (technological) [S8][R1].

*Visual (chart):* Research methods + findings summary

> Talk track: Emphasize: this is real coded review data, not vibes - 500 reviews, 53 one-stars, every theme counted.

## Slide 17 — VII. Proposed Budget: Proposed Budget & Financial Viability
- Total $21,000: media $8,000 [A4]; creators 8 x $800 [A6]; events 8 x $300 [A7]; inserts [A9]; tools [A10]; contingency [A11].
- Funnel: 1.2M x 1.5% x 0.6% = ~108 orders x $950 = $102.6K [A1][A4].
- GP 32% = $32.8K; ROI ~56% [A2]. Breakeven ~69 orders [O1].
- Conservative (40%): ~43 orders -> -$7K, offset by 800 captures (~$12K list value) + lower support cost [A12][A13].
- Margin sensitivity: 25% GP -> -14%; 40% GP -> +95% (ebike hardware range) [A2].

*Visual (chart):* Budget breakdown + ROI scenarios

> Talk track: Line-item every dollar; show the conservative case honestly and how email list + support savings mitigate.

## Slide 18 — VIII. Impact & Measurement: Impact & Measurement
