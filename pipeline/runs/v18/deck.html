I. Overview

Campaign Overview

  • Thesis: PUCKIPUPPY's product wins - its delivery journey loses buyers. 11% of 500 reviews are 1-star; ~68% of those cite shipping damage, assembly, app issues, or support [R1][S5].
  • Big idea: Arrive Ready - every order ships with a damage-claim promise, QR setup concierge, and finance/trade-in path; trust badges move above the fold [R6].
  • Demo: judges sit on the Labrador Pro and ride - the product demos itself [S2].
  • Goal: 90 attributed orders + 400 test rides + cut damage-review share in 45 days.

II. Product & Company

The Product

  • PUCKIPUPPY makes dog-breed-named ebikes - Labrador Pro (~$1,399), Boxer ST, Corgi, Husky trike - for riders 28-50 who want car-free trips [S2].
  • UL 2849/2271 certified; torque sensor praised as 'natural' feel; 80-mile range in reviews [S2][S5].
  • Problem: $1,299-$1,699 sticker + freight shipping + self-assembly = the highest-anxiety purchase in the category [R1][R4].

VI. Strategy, Activities & Schedule

Campaign Strategy

  • PESO: Paid (geo social/search) + Earned (ebike press push) + Owned (email/SMS, site trust dashboard) + Shared (creator UGC) [G1-G4].
  • Positioning: 'the ebike that arrives ready' - own the delivery-experience gap no competitor claims [R1][R6].
  • Message house: proof (UL, 4.7 Trustpilot, ride feel) -> protection (Arrive Ready) -> pathway (test ride, finance).
  • Measurement: codes + UTMs on every program; weekly KPI review; pre/post review-corpus recount [R1].

VII. Proposed Budget

Proposed Budget & Financial Viability

  • Total $21,000: media $8,000 [A4]; creators 8 x $800 [A6]; events 8 x $300 [A7]; inserts [A9]; tools [A10]; contingency [A11].
  • Funnel: 1.2M x 1.5% x 0.6% = ~108 orders x $950 = $102.6K [A1][A4].
  • GP 32% = $32.8K; ROI ~56% [A2]. Breakeven ~69 orders [O1].
  • Conservative (40%): ~43 orders -> -$7K, offset by 800 captures (~$12K list value) + lower support cost [A12][A13].
  • Margin sensitivity: 25% GP -> -14%; 40% GP -> +95% (ebike hardware range) [A2].

VIII. Impact & Measurement

Impact & Measurement
