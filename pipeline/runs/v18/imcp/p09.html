METHODOLOGY & FINDINGS
Research Method & Results
METHODS
Secondary (n=8): company about/press/product pages, Trustpilot, Tom's Guide, third-party store metrics, industry data — complete
Review Analysis (n=500): public Judge.me review API on puckipuppy.com; keyword-coded content analysis (analyze_reviews.py); 1-star deep-dive — complete
Digital Shelf Audit (n=6): public web audit: product/price pages, retailer locator, certifications, competitor price check — complete
KEY RESULTS
n=500 reviews: avg 4.31/5, 70% five-star, 11% one-star (53). Of 1-star reviews, ~68% cite shipping damage, difficult assembly, app/connectivity, or slow support - not ride quality
Positive drivers: torque-sensor ride feel, 80-mile range, fast delivery, easy assembly - the product itself satisfies buyers who receive it intact
Only ~2% of reviews mention repeat/bulk purchase; gifting 3% - consideration-to-repeat path is thin for a $1.4K ticket
DTC-only premium positioning ($1,299-$1,699 hero SKUs) vs. 'affordable for all' brand promise; no mass retail presence found in audit
Founder-led dog-companion story (Bob + Puck, est. 2022) is distinctive and press-covered, but underused vs. generic ebike competitors
UL 2849/2271 certifications and Trustpilot 4.7/620 are strong trust assets that are buried below the fold on product pages
