Campaign Overview
THESIS
Stryve does not need more doors - it needs shoppers who try it once to come back.
PROBLEM
net sales fell ~41% to $17.7M (FY2023) while distribution grew - a velocity problem, per its own 10-K.
OPPORTUNITY
29% of 1,400 reviews praise taste; 20% reorder - the product converts once tried.
SOLUTION
'It's Not Jerky' puts Braav in the jerky shopper's decision at DFW H-E-Bs and online.
45-DAY GOAL
2,000 attributed purchases, +25% units/store vs. control.
