## Slide 4 — I. Overview: Campaign Overview
- Thesis: Pan's won its Shark Tank pitch - now Texas students pitch Pan's [S4].
- Problem: Pan's grew from ~750 to 2,500+ stores, yet revenue fell from $3.15M (FY22) with losses every year - shoppers are not trying it [S1][S2][S4].
- Business opportunity: meat snacks +11.3% to $6.4B while plant-based meat fell 10% - flavor-curious Gen Z needs a reason to see Pan's as a snack, not fake meat [S6][S7].
- Solution: 'Pan's Tank' invites Texas students to pitch the next flavor Shark Tank-style - and taste Pan's at H-E-B to do it [O1].
- 45-day goal: 5,000 attributed purchases and 1,500 flavor pitches [O1][O2].

*Visual (table):* Four boxes: Problem / Business opportunity / Solution / 45-day goal

> Talk track: Open with the paradox: Pan's went from about 750 stores to 2,500, yet revenue fell. The problem is not distribution - it is what happens at the shelf.

## Slide 5 — II. Product & Company: The Product
- Shiitake mushroom jerky from a decades-old Pan family recipe found in Malaysia; chewy, savory, umami [S2].
- Vegan, gluten-free, Kosher, no allergens to declare; the brand says it 'isn't trying to completely imitate beef' [S5].
- Shelf price $7.99-$8.99 per 2.2 oz bag; cost to make about $1.67 per bag at launch [S3].
- Four core flavors; sold online, on Amazon, and in grocery including H-E-B in Texas [S2][S10].

*Visual (image):* Flat-lay of four flavor bags with a torn-open bag showing the jerky texture

> Talk track: Stress the brand's own words: it is not trying to be beef. That line becomes our whole positioning.

## Slide 6 — II. Product & Company: The Company & Its Position
- Founded by engineer Michael Pan; launched 2018; Mark Cuban invested $300K for 18% on Shark Tank in 2020 [S3][S4].
- Revenue $3.15M (FY22) -> $2.88M (FY23) -> $2.78M (11 months FY24); net loss every year [S1].
- Store count rose from about 750 to 2,500+ while revenue fell - fewer bags sold per store [S1][S2][S4].
- Category tailwind: meat snacks +11.3% to $6.4B; headwind: plant-based meat -10% [S6][S7].

*Visual (chart):* Line chart: revenue FY22-FY24 falling vs. bar chart of store count rising; category map with meat snacks up and plant-based meat down

> Talk track: This is our most important slide for credibility: every number comes from the company's own SEC filing.

## Slide 8 — IV. Target Market: Target Market

- P2 Flexitarian H-E-B Parents, 30-50: weekly H-E-B shoppers who use app coupons and want allergen-free snacks [S5][S9].
- P1 creates the buzz; P2 buys the bags at the shelf during demo weekends.
- Market: 6 in 10 US households already buy plant-based foods [S11].

## Slide 9 — V. Research, Insights & Connection: Research Method & Results
- Secondary (complete): SEC filing, GFI/SPINS, Circana via trade press, company pages - 11 sources [S1][S6][S7][S8].
- Key results: revenue down while stores up [S1][S2]; meat snacks +11.3% [S7]; plant-based meat -10% [S6]; Chomps +61.5% shows new snack brands can break out [S8].
- Primary (in field): 10-question survey of Central Texas snack buyers, 3-5 interviews with taste test, H-E-B shelf audit at 3 stores.
- Influences: creator culture (social), plant-based fatigue (cultural), food inflation (economic), short video (technological) [S6][S7].

*Visual (chart):* Methods table + category growth bar chart

> Talk track: Methods first, then results.

## Slide 10 — V. Research, Insights & Connection: Insights -> Campaign Decisions
- Finding: store count up, revenue down [S1][S2] -> therefore every entry path requires tasting at H-E-B or online with a coupon.
- Finding: plant-based meat -10%, meat snacks +11.3% [S6][S7] -> therefore pitches are about flavor and snacking, never 'fake meat.'
- Finding: founder won on Shark Tank [S3][S4] -> therefore the contest borrows the Shark Tank format the brand truly owns.
- Finding: losses every year [S1] -> therefore prizes are low-cost and the winner's flavor goes to R&D after the campaign.

*Visual (table):* Finding -> therefore rows with source tags

> Talk track: Survey results will add R1-R3 here.

## Slide 13 — VI. Strategy, Activities & Schedule: Program 1: Pitch Your Flavor Open Call
- Pitch Your Flavor goes live: Texas students 14+ film a 30-second Shark Tank-style pitch for a new Pan's flavor [O2][O3].
- No purchase necessary, official rules posted, parental consent under 18.
- Every entrant receives a 'buy 2 save $3' coupon to taste before they pitch [O1].
- Target: 1,500 pitches and 400 public videos [O2][O3].

*Visual (mockup):* TikTok mockup: student holding a Pan's bag pitching 'Hill Country Smoke' with on-screen 'Pitch Your Flavor' sticker

## Slide 14 — VI. Strategy, Activities & Schedule: Program 2: Taste-Before-You-Pitch Demo Days
- Pitch Your Flavor in store: 16 weekend demo shifts at 8 Central Texas H-E-B stores [O1][O2].
- Shoppers taste current flavors, scan for $3 off 2 bags, and can submit a pitch on a card [A7][A8].
- Why: revenue fell while stores grew - trial at the shelf is the missing step [S1][S2].
- Target: 3,200 samples; sales lift measured vs. matched control stores [O1].

*Visual (mockup):* Demo table mockup with 'Taste. Then Pitch.' sign, sample cups, QR coupon

## Slide 17 — VII. Proposed Budget: Proposed Budget & Financial Viability
- Total $15,000: paid social $5,500 [A4]; creators $3,000 [A6]; demos 16 x $150 = $2,400 [A7]; finals $1,500 [A12]; coupon $1,500 [A8]; production $800 [A9]; tools $300 [A10].
- Funnel: 4M impressions x 1.2% CTR x 11% purchase = 5,280 purchases x $8 brand revenue = $42.2K [A1][A4].
- Gross profit at 42% = $17.7K; profit ROI 18%; ROAS 4.97x on $8.5K ad spend [A2].
- Break-even 4,464 purchases. Conservative case (50%) 2,640 loses $6.1K in-window - offset by 6,000 owned subscribers and a new-flavor pipeline [O2].

*Visual (table):* Itemized budget + funnel math + base vs. conservative case

> Talk track: Lower in-window ROI than a pure trial program; the payoff is the owned audience and a product idea.
