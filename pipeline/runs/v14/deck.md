- Opportunity: 'the unkillable plant' is botanically true, demo-able live, and unclaimed by competitors [R5].
- Solution: trial sampling + a public 'School Plant' impact counter + corporate-gift push on existing wholesale rails [O1][O3].

## Slide 12 — VI. Strategy, Activities & Schedule: Program 1: The Unkillable Plant Trials
- The Unkillable Plant in person: 6 weekend sampling shifts at Austin nurseries, plant shops, and farmers markets; control venues get the code only [O1][O2].
- Mechanic: every sampled plant carries a dated care tag - 'neglect test: survives 30 days forgotten'; QR to 'first plant $5 off' + email capture; ~150 samples/shift = 900 total [A7][A8].
- Why: the product converts on first sight but shoppers do not know what it is - live plants + the neglect tag make the claim physical [R1][R5].
- Measured: QR scans per venue and sample-to-purchase vs. control [O1].

*Visual (mockup):* Sampling table mockup: plants on cork bark, 'The Unkillable Plant' banner, care-tag close-up, QR sign

> Talk track: Interactive: hand the judge a live plant with the care tag. 'This survives 30 days of your neglect. Try forgetting it exists.'
