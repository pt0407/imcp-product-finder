I. Overview

Campaign Overview

  • Thesis: Air Plant Supply Co does not need more products - it needs Americans to know a plant can survive their neglect, and every living plant sold builds a school in Guatemala [O1].
  • Problem: shoppers cannot identify air plants - the product needs education before the sale, and the brand's best asset (Pencils of Promise schools) is invisible, earning $1/order with zero marketing [R1][R2].
  • Opportunity: 'the unkillable plant' is botanically true, demo-able live, and unclaimed by competitors [R5].
  • Solution: trial sampling + a public 'School Plant' impact counter + corporate-gift push on existing wholesale rails [O1][O3].
  • 45-day goal: 600 attributed purchases and a running donation counter judges can watch [O1].

VI. Strategy, Activities & Schedule

Campaign Strategy

  • Big idea: The Unkillable Plant - the only plant that survives your neglect AND builds a school while it does. Message: 'Kill-proof. Care-free. World-changing.'
  • Positioning: for people who want plants without the learning curve - prove it alive, then give it purpose [R5][R2].
  • IMC mix: Paid (geo social) + Earned (Pencils of Promise/Guatemala story) + Shared (#UnkillablePlant tags) + Owned (email/SMS, care tags, School Plant page).
  • Push-pull: B2B prospects get the corporate plant story (push); shoppers get trials + mission (pull) [O1][O3].

VI. Strategy, Activities & Schedule

Program 1: The Unkillable Plant Trials

  • The Unkillable Plant in person: 6 weekend sampling shifts at Austin nurseries, plant shops, and farmers markets; control venues get the code only [O1][O2].
  • Mechanic: every sampled plant carries a dated care tag - 'neglect test: survives 30 days forgotten'; QR to 'first plant $5 off' + email capture; ~150 samples/shift = 900 total [A7][A8].
  • Why: the product converts on first sight but shoppers do not know what it is - live plants + the neglect tag make the claim physical [R1][R5].
  • Measured: QR scans per venue and sample-to-purchase vs. control [O1].
