Campaign Overview
THESIS
Air Plant Supply Co does not need more products - it needs Americans to know a plant can survive their neglect, and every living plant sold builds a school in Guatemala.
PROBLEM
shoppers cannot identify air plants - the product needs education before the sale, and the brand's best asset (Pencils of Promise schools) is invisible, earning $1/order with zero marketing.
OPPORTUNITY
'the unkillable plant' is botanically true, demo-able live, and unclaimed by competitors.
SOLUTION
trial sampling + a public 'School Plant' impact counter + corporate-gift push on existing wholesale rails.
45-DAY GOAL
600 attributed purchases and a running donation counter judges can watch.
