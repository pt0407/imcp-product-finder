## Slide 4 — I. Overview: Campaign Overview
- Problem: Boba Tea Protein sells 100% online, but the boba occasion happens inside shops - 11,162 US shops and 1,269 in Texas where the brand has zero presence [S2][S5].
- Business opportunity: protein RTD sales jumped 13% to $7.6B in 2025 while bubble tea grows ~11% a year - 'protein boba' is an unclaimed intersection, and The Guardian just named the brand best overall [S1][S4][S6].
- Solution: 'Sip Stronger' puts protein boba inside Texas boba shops for sampling, then converts that trial into DTC orders through a UGC challenge and creator seeding [O1].
- 45-day goal: 1,500 new orders and 6,000 new email/SMS subscribers [O1][O2].

*Visual (table):* Four boxes: Problem / Business opportunity / Solution / 45-day goal

> Talk track: Open with the shop statistic: the customer buys boba in a shop six days a week, but this brand only exists on a website. That is the structural problem we are solving.

- Finding: protein RTD is the fastest-growing supplement format and 'protein boba' has no dominant brand [S4][S6] -> therefore the campaign owns the phrase 'protein boba' with #MyProteinBoba (G2).
- Finding: Texas is the #2 boba state and the brand is headquartered in Dallas [S2][S7] -> therefore the hero push is Texas-first, starting in DFW shops.
- Finding: The Guardian's taste test is fresh third-party proof [S6] -> therefore PR re-amplifies the 'best overall' result instead of inventing claims.
- Finding (pending survey): channel and price-preference data will re-weight paid media vs. shop spend in week 1 [R-pending].

## Slide 11 — VI. Strategy, Activities & Schedule: Campaign Strategy
- Big idea 'Sip Stronger': turn the weekly boba run into a protein upgrade - try it at the shop, post it, sip it stronger.
- IMC mix: Paid (TikTok/Meta) + Earned (Guardian proof, PR) + Shared (UGC challenge) + Owned (email/SMS, shop codes, POS cards).
- Push-pull: shops get sampling and margin (push); consumers get creator proof and a challenge (pull); both point to the same offer: five dollars off the first order [O1].
- Ownable element competitors cannot copy: founder Neil Hoang's trainer story plus the only 'best overall' protein-boba taste-test win [S5][S6].

*Visual (image):* Key visual: split image - regular boba cup vs. Protein Matcha tub, 'Sip Stronger' lockup

> Talk track: Theme check: 'Sip Stronger' appears on every activity slide. That consistency is what makes a campaign feel like one idea, not five tactics.

## Slide 13 — VI. Strategy, Activities & Schedule: Program 1: Sip at the Shop
- Sip Stronger goes physical: 20 independent Texas boba shops sample Protein Matcha for 7 weeks [O1][O2].
- Mechanic: free 2-oz sample cup + counter card with shop-specific QR code + a five-dollar-off-first-order offer.
- Why it works: 1,269 Texas shops already own the boba occasion [S2]; we rent their foot traffic instead of buying cold traffic.
- Measurement: per-shop QR codes attribute every order; target 25% of campaign orders from shop codes [A7].

*Visual (mockup):* Counter card mockup: 'Try Protein Matcha - Sip Stronger' + QR code + $5 off

## Slide 17 — VII. Proposed Budget: Proposed Budget & Financial Viability
- Total campaign budget: $20,000 across media, creators, sampling, production, tools, PR, and 10% contingency [A4][A6][A7][A8][A9][A10][A11][A12].
- Funnel math: 3.5M impressions x 1.2% CTR = 42K clicks x 4% conversion = 1,680 orders x $50 AOV = $84K revenue [A1][A4].
- Gross profit at 62% margin = $52K; profit-based ROI = 160%; ROAS = 6.0x on $14K ad spend [A2].
- Break-even = 645 orders; base case 1,680, conservative case (50%) 840 orders still clears break-even [O1].
- CAC = $11.90 base / $23.81 conservative vs. $50 AOV - healthy payback inside the campaign window [A1][A2].

*Visual (table):* Line-item budget table + funnel math + base/conservative case table

> Talk track: Lead with break-even: the campaign pays for itself at 645 orders, and our conservative case is 840. That is a financial answer, not a hope.
