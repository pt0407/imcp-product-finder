Campaign Overview
PROBLEM
Boba Tea Protein sells 100% online, but the boba occasion happens inside shops - 11,162 US shops and 1,269 in Texas where the brand has zero presence.
BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY
protein RTD sales jumped 13% to $7.6B in 2025 while bubble tea grows ~11% a year - 'protein boba' is an unclaimed intersection, and The Guardian just named the brand best overall.
SOLUTION
'Sip Stronger' puts protein boba inside Texas boba shops for sampling, then converts that trial into DTC orders through a UGC challenge and creator seeding.
45-DAY GOAL
1,500 new orders and 6,000 new email/SMS subscribers.
