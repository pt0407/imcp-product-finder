Campaign Overview
THESIS
Pan's does not need more stores - it needs more bags sold per store.
PROBLEM
stores grew from ~750 to 2,500+ while revenue fell from $3.15M (FY22) - at most ~6 bags per store per week.
OPPORTUNITY
meat snacks +11.3% to $6.4B while plant-based meat fell 10% - Pan's sits in the wrong mental aisle.
SOLUTION
'Switch to Mushroom Mode' puts Pan's in the jerky shopper's decision at Houston/Austin/San Antonio H-E-Bs and online.
45-DAY GOAL
1,500 attributed purchases and +25% units per store vs. control - the test still runs if the clip strip is declined.
