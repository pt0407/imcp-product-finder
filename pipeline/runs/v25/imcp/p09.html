METHODOLOGY & FINDINGS
Research Methodology & Results
METHODS
Local Review Analysis (n=250): ~250 public Google/Yelp reviews of the actual Austin Fleet Feet store, keyword-coded — complete
Digital Shelf Audit (n=8): Fleet Feet site + Austin store pages, fit process, Happy Fit guarantee, vs DTC/Amazon — complete
Academic Industry Review (n=6): Peer-reviewed studies on shoe-fit injury + RunningUSA participation data + Strava year-in-sport — complete
KEY RESULTS
~90% of new runners quit within a year; academic studies link wrong-fit shoes to injury-driven dropout - the fit problem is the category's retention killer
Of 250 coded Austin-store reviews: 47 name the gait analysis/fit, 31 praise staff expertise, and verbatim quotes read like 'they watched me walk and fixed my knee pain' -
Local reviews + event listings show a real run community exists but is invisible to non-customers - the asset is there, the awareness isn't
DTC wins on convenience and price; the store's only durable edge is relationship + fit - confirmed by industry and local data
