I. Overview

Campaign Overview

  • Problem: the 2026 KeHE Golden Ticket opens nationwide doors NOW, but with 2 employees and DTC-only sales, nothing on shelf says 'this is not the jerky you hate' [S3][R5].

II. Product & Company

The Product

  • Crunchy Jerky Chips: beef (48g protein) and pork (46g protein) - 0g sugar, ~230 cal, crunch 'through and through' via patent-pending process [S1][S9].
  • Charcuterie Trail Mix: pork jerky chips + parmesan crisps + roasted almonds - 52g protein, 0g added sugar, 7g fiber; 'a charcuterie board in a bag' [S1].
  • Non-UPF Verified, BRCGS Grade AA facility, Made in USA; ~$10-13/bag online, multipacks for retail [S1].
  • Sold DTC, Amazon, Austin/California independents; nationwide via KeHE (Golden Ticket) + Faire wholesale [S1][S3].

Insights -> Campaign Decisions

VI. Strategy, Activities & Schedule

Program 1: Crunch-Test Demo Days

  • Crunch, Verified in person: 8 weekend demo shifts at Austin fitness studios, farmers markets, and partner retail; control stores get the code only [O1][O2].
  • Mechanic: blind crunch-off - BEEST vs. the jerky shoppers actually buy; judged by sound and texture; QR to '$3 off 2 bags'; ~200 samples per shift = 1,600 total [A7][A8].
  • Why: the product converts jerky-haters on first bite - 'I HATE jerky but LOVE Beest' - and most shoppers have never heard of it [R1][R2][R5].
  • Measured: QR scans per venue and sample-to-purchase vs. control [O1].
