## Slide 4 — I. Overview: Campaign Overview
- Thesis: All Y'Alls does not need more doors - it needs shoppers who try it once to come back [O1].
- Problem: 450+ stores and 6 years in H-E-B, yet Amazon shoppers rate it 3.0-4.2 vs. 4.95 on the brand's own site [S1][S5][R5][R6].
- Opportunity: 73% of site reviewers praise taste unprompted - the product converts; the problem is reaching meat-snack shoppers [R1].
- Solution: 'The Smarter Switch' puts All Y'Alls in the jerky shopper's decision at DFW H-E-Bs and online [O1].
- 45-day goal: 1,500 attributed purchases and +25% units per store vs. control - the test still runs if the clip strip is declined [O1].

## Slide 5 — II. Product & Company: The Product
- Plant-protein jerky ('The Smarter Jerky') made from whole non-GMO soybeans; 15-17g protein + 8-9g fiber per 2.69 oz bag, 0mg cholesterol [S1].
- Four bold Texas flavors: Jalapeño BBQ, Black Pepper & Sea Salt, Prickly Pear Teriyaki, Prickly Pear Chipotle - each named for a Texas region [S1].
- Shelf price ~$8/bag, 3-packs $24 online; sold DTC, on Amazon, and in 450+ retail doors incl. H-E-B, Kroger, 7-Eleven, Whole Foods [S1][S2][S4].
- Backed by Dr. Rizwan Bukhari, a Texas vascular surgeon and PCRM member, who is Medical Director AND investor; portion of every sale supports Rowdy Girl Sanctuary [S5].

*Visual (image):* Flat-lay of the four flavor bags with a torn-open bag showing jerky texture

> Talk track: Stress the texture reviews keep praising - 'texture is spot on' - because that is what wins over meat jerky buyers.

## Slide 6 — II. Product & Company: The Company & Its Position
- Founded by Brett Christoffel in Cross Roads, TX (2018); ~6 employees; 600,000+ bags sold; bootstrapped [S1][S2][S3].
- 6+ years in H-E-B; won 1st place plant-based / 6th of 830 in H-E-B Quest for Texas Best 2019; sales +104% across 138 H-E-B stores in 2020-21 [S2].
- Competitors: Jack Link's, Chomps, Perchik's own plant-jerky set - All Y'Alls is the only Texas-born national plant jerky [S8].
- Position: jerky upgrade for everyone - plant protein with the chew and bold flavor of jerky, not a 'vegan alternative' [S1][R2].

## Slide 9 — V. Research, Insights & Connection: Research Method & Results
- Secondary (complete): company site, StartupCPG interview, LinkedIn, Faire wholesale, Amazon store page - 10 sources [S1][S2][S3][S4][S5].
- Primary, no-survey (complete): keyword-coded analysis of 139 public site reviews [R1][R4] and a public distribution/digital-shelf audit of 8 sources [R5][R6] [S12]; survey/interviews not run - reviews skew positive (self-selected buyers).
- Key results: taste dominates reviews, complaints only 6% [R1]; 37% cite diet/vegan context but the praise is texture [R2]; only 2% found it in a store [R3]; H-E-B relationship strong but Amazon ratings lag a full point [R5][R6].
- Influences: protein-snack boom (social), flexitarian movement (cultural), food inflation (economic), retail app coupons (technological) [S6][S7][S9].

*Visual (image):* 

> Talk track: Name the methods and sample sizes out loud. The Amazon-vs-site rating gap is the finding judges will remember.

## Slide 10 — V. Research, Insights & Connection: Insights -> Campaign Decisions
- Finding: 73% of 139 reviews volunteer taste unprompted; complaints are only 6% [R1] -> demos and taste-offs lead, not awareness ads.
- Finding: only 2% discovered it in a store; most buyers find it online [R3] -> retail trial must be manufactured at the shelf.
- Finding: Amazon 3.0-4.2 vs. site 4.95 - a full-point channel gap [R5][R6] -> generate 'texture is spot on' proof where shoppers look.
- Finding: 37% cite vegan/diet context but 5% compare it to meat jerky [R2] -> sell it as a jerky upgrade, not a vegan product.
- Finding: 19% reorder and price complaints are 1% [R4] -> end with a bundle/repeat offer, not a discount.

## Slide 11 — VI. Strategy, Activities & Schedule: Campaign Strategy
- Big idea: The Smarter Switch - jerky lovers don't have to give up jerky to eat smarter. Message: 'Love jerky? Switch to the Smarter Jerky.'
- Positioning: for jerky shoppers who want the same chew and bold flavor with plant protein, fiber, and no cholesterol, All Y'Alls is the upgrade [S1][R2].
- IMC mix: Paid (geo social) + Earned (Texas founder story + surgeon-investor) + Shared (#SmarterSwitch taste-off duets) + Owned (email/SMS, shelf talkers).
- Push-pull: H-E-B gets a measurable velocity test (push); shoppers get samples and a coupon (pull) [O1][O2].

*Visual (image):* Key visual: split frame - beef jerky aisle on left, All Y'Alls bag glowing on right, 'The Smarter Switch' lockup

> Talk track: Point to the positioning statement. 'Not a vegan product - a jerky upgrade' is the line a competitor can't copy because their brand is built on the niche.

## Slide 12 — VI. Strategy, Activities & Schedule: Program 1: Smarter Switch Demo Days
- The Smarter Switch in person: 12 weekend demo shifts at 6 DFW H-E-B test stores; 6 control stores get the coupon only [O1][O2].
- Mechanic: sample cups at the jerky/meat-snack set; QR code to '$2 off 2 bags'; 200 samples per shift = 2,400 total [A7][A8].
- Why: 73% of reviewers say taste is why they buy - and only 2% found it in a store, so the shelf is where discovery must happen [R1][R3].
- Measured: QR scans per store and units per store vs. control stores [O1].

## Slide 17 — VII. Proposed Budget: Proposed Budget & Financial Viability
- Total $10,000: paid social $4,000 [A4]; creators 3 x $750 = $2,250 [A6]; demos 12 x $200 = $2,400 [A7]; production $700 [A9]; tools $300 [A10]; contingency $350 [A11].
- Funnel: 1.0M impressions x 1.5% x 10% = 1,500 purchases; half H-E-B coupon (~$6 net), half online 3-packs (~$20 net) = $20 avg, $30K [A1][A4].
- The $2 coupon is manufacturer-funded, already netted in blended revenue - no separate line [A1][A8].
- Gross profit at 55% = $16.5K; profit ROI 65%; ROAS 5.0x on $6.25K ad spend [A2].
- Conservative (50%): 750 purchases, ROI ~-18%; 30% repeat in 90 days reaches ~break-even [A13]. Upside: ~480 demo-day purchases [A15].

> Talk track: Lead with honesty: in-window it breaks even; the return comes from repeat buyers and from fixing the Amazon rating gap. Then show the test-vs-control design that proves it.
