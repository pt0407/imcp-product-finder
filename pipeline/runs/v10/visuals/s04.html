Campaign Overview
THESIS
All Y'Alls does not need more doors - it needs shoppers who try it once to come back.
PROBLEM
450+ stores and 6 years in H-E-B, yet Amazon shoppers rate it 3.0-4.2 vs. 4.95 on the brand's own site - and most Amazon listings show 'currently unavailable'.
OPPORTUNITY
73% of site reviewers praise taste unprompted - the product converts; the problem is reaching meat-snack shoppers where they shop.
SOLUTION
'The Smarter Switch' puts All Y'Alls in the jerky shopper's decision at DFW H-E-Bs and online.
45-DAY GOAL
1,500 attributed purchases and +25% units per store vs. control - the test still runs if the clip strip is declined.
