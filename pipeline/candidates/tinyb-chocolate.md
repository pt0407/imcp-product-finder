**Total: 26/40**

## One-sentence business problem
tinyB grows through corporate workshops but only ~14% of revenue comes from consumer gifting — the DTC channel that compounds is underbuilt.

## Campaign angle to test first
"Borogodó, Delivered" — convert corporate workshop hosts into consumer gifting ambassadors via a kit insert + host referral program.

## Top open questions
1. Real DTC order volume and repeat rate (not in the investor brief).
2. Will tinyB put an insert card in corporate event kits?
3. Actual AOV and gross margin for the DTC line.

## Verdict: MAYBE
Strong strategy and an ownable idea, but research feasibility (2/5) is disqualifying on this timeline: the team cannot reach tinyB's customers, visit a store, or build a review corpus. A deck for tinyB is capped near the same score as Pan's while carrying more execution risk. Revisit only if the team has a direct line to the company or its customers.
