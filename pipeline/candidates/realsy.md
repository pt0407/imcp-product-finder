## One-sentence business problem
Realsy scales a single-product snack to 3,000+ stores, but Amazon shoppers rate it a full point below DTC buyers — the brand hasn't converted mass retail trial into consistent conviction.

## Campaign angle to test first
Flavor-line expansion + trial program: convert the date platform into a snacking brand (PB&J, chocolate) while fixing the Amazon quality-perception gap.

## Top open questions
1. What's driving the Amazon-vs-DTC rating gap (stale inventory, co-packer variance)?
2. Reorder rate across the 3,000 doors.
3. Costco launch status and terms.

## Verdict: MAYBE/BUILD
Strong DFW story and real revenue, but the "problem" is softer than Stryve/All Y'Alls — growth is the headline, not decline. Best used if judges reward momentum stories; the rating-gap angle is the sharpest hook.
