# Fleet Feet — scorecard + build notes (2026-11-08)

## The business
- Running-specialty retail franchise; ~250 stores nationally, **real Austin TX location** (District 5 geography — local judges may shop it)
- Model: free in-store gait analysis + 60-day "Happy Fit" guarantee + weekly group runs/clinics
- Competes on service/community, not price — vs DTC/Amazon convenience

## Why it scores
| Factor | Status |
|---|---|
| Local angle | **Elite** — Austin store + Texas district judges + community the judges can join |
| Corpus | ~250 Google/Yelp reviews of the *actual Austin store* (coded: 47 name gait/fit, 31 praise staff) |
| Documented problem | Real: new runners quit (~90%/yr, academic injury-fit link) + store's best asset invisible to online-first buyers |
| Prop | Compliant — running shoe handed around; judges can join a real Saturday run |
| Founder/owner access | Franchise local owner — interviewable, like Chisos |
| Founder story | "We fixed your knee" service stories in local reviews |

## Weaknesses (honest)
- Franchise, not indie brand — "brand already does runs/clinics" objection (mitigated: campaign = amplification + QR attribution + challenge IP, not invention)
- Negative direct ROI (-32%) — defended via 12-month LTV (+28%) and community asset persistence
- Not publicly traded — no SEC filings (unlike Stryve/Allbirds)
