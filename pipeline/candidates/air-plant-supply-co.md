**Total: 30/40** — fails Build-it rule on criterion 1 (needs ≥4). Borderline MAYBE.

## One-sentence business problem
Air Plant Supply Co. sells a product most Americans can't identify — the category needs education before the sale, and the brand's $1-per-order school mission is its most differentiating asset but is barely marketed.

## Campaign angle to test first
"The Unkillable Plant" — position air plants as the plant for people who kill plants; pair trial/demo sampling with the Pencils of Promise mission ("every plant builds a school") and measure trial→repeat + wholesale inquiries.

## Top open questions
1. What share of revenue is Q4 vs. Q1? (Seasonality determines whether the campaign should defend the cliff or grow the base.)
2. Does the Pencils of Promise partnership have a formal agreement/brand usage rights?
3. Any retail wholesale doors beyond DTC drop-shipping?

## Verdict: MAYBE (user override → building)
Honest read: weakest evidence base of any deck candidate — zero reviews, no revenue data, no storefront. But the mission story (real partner, named communities, live product judges can hold) is the most winner-pattern-aligned concept found outside food. Build it; expect Research to cap low unless the team runs real intercept research.
