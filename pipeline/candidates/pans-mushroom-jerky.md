## One-sentence business problem
Pan's tripled its retail footprint while revenue fell for three straight years — each store sells fewer bags, and shoppers walking past the shelf don't pick it up.

## Campaign angle to test first
"Mushroom Mode" — reposition from plant-based meat alternative to honest savory snack beside jerky; in-store demos + app coupon at Central Texas H-E-Bs, measured by test/control sell-through.

## Top open questions
1. Actual H-E-B sell-through / velocity per store (SEC filing doesn't break it out).
2. Wholesale price and margin to retailer (needed for promo economics).
3. Whether H-E-B will approve sampling/demo days and the app coupon.

## Verdict: BUILD IT
Best-evidenced candidate on the board. The problem is provable without the company, research is fully executable by the team in one week, and the measurement design is airtight.
