## One-sentence business problem
Oca Foods is in 40+ H-E-B stores but nobody knows its name — distribution without awareness, and the founder says so himself.

## Campaign angle to test first
"Taste of Brazil" sampling + story-driven trial at Austin H-E-Bs, converting paçoca curiosity into repeat purchase.

## Top open questions
1. Actual revenue and H-E-B velocity.
2. Can review corpus reach n≥100 pooled across SKUs?
3. Austin FC/Q2 grant terms (co-marketing opportunity?).

## Verdict: MAYBE
Beautiful underdog story with an H-E-B shelf already secured, but the awareness-only problem and sub-$1M scale make it a smaller canvas than Stryve/All Y'Alls. Strong third option.
