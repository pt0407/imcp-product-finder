## One-sentence business problem
All Y'Alls moved plant jerky into 450+ stores and 600K bags, but Amazon shoppers rate it 3.8-4.2 while the brand claims 4.98 — the product isn't converting trial into conviction outside its own site.

## Campaign angle to test first
"Jerky, Reimagined" mainstream trial push: in-store demos + honest taste content converting flexitarians, measured by Amazon rating velocity + H-E-B sell-through in test stores.

## Top open questions
1. Why the Amazon-vs-site rating gap — texture, stale inventory, or review gating?
2. H-E-B door count and per-store velocity.
3. Which of the 450+ doors are actually reordered vs. one-time placements.

## Verdict: BUILD IT
Texas jerky with the same H-E-B measurement playbook as Pan's, a documented channel-quality problem, and almost no marketing sophistication. Slightly softer evidence than Stryve but a better "agency adds value" story.
