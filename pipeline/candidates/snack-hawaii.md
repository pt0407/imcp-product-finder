## One-sentence business problem
Snack Hawaii's wholesale/retail distribution is strong but its DTC channel is collapsing (est. -50% YoY) — the brand needs an owned-audience growth engine it doesn't have.

## Campaign angle to test first
A "Taste of Hawaii" subscription-first push: bundling bestsellers into a trial box with creator unboxings, measured by subscription conversion.

## Top open questions
1. Verify the Grips DTC decline figure (third-party estimate — confirm direction on their analytics or similarweb).
2. Subscription box attach rate and churn.
3. Whether wholesale accounts (Walmart/Costco) would support a cross-merch display.

## Verdict: MAYBE
Real evidenced problem and a workable DTC research path, but no Texas relevance and a weaker ownable mechanic than Pan's retail-velocity play. Backup candidate only.
