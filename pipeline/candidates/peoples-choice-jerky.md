## One-sentence business problem
A scaled jerky brand (~$11.5M, factory production) is concentrated on Amazon while its own-site traffic falls — it needs a channel beyond Amazon it hasn't built.

## Campaign angle to test first
Heritage-driven DTC push: "Since 1929" storytelling + flavor-heritage content converting Amazon browsers to the brand's own subscription.

## Top open questions
1. DTC vs. Amazon revenue split (not public).
2. Whether they have retail distribution beyond their one LA store.
3. Review corpus quality — are the 30K reviews verified-purchase?

## Verdict: MAYBE
The review-analysis research path is the strongest available (30K reviews), but the brand's existing marketing sophistication undercuts the "agency adds value" story that wins IMCP. Pan's still leads.
