# Digital shelf / distribution audit: All Y'Alls Foods
Date: 2026-11-07. Method: public web audit of brand site, locator page, wholesale listings, and press.

## Findings
- Brand claims (allyallsfoods.com, homepage + store-locator page): "450+ retail locations across the U.S."; locator powered by Stockist; "contact us" fallback when no store found near you.
- H-E-B: 6+ year relationship; 1st place plant-based proteins / 6th of 830 in H-E-B Quest for Texas Best 2019; "grew sales 104% from 2020 to 2021 in 138 of their stores" (StartupCPG founder interview).
- Distribution partners named in StartupCPG interview: KeHE, UNFI, Mabel, Faire, H-E-B, Kroger, 7-Eleven; plus Amazon (US and Europe per LinkedIn).
- Wholesale: Faire listing for Prickly Pear Teriyaki 6-pack at $65.88 MSRP, 5.0 rating (12 reviews).
- Product line: 4 jerky flavors (Jalapeño BBQ, Black Pepper & Sea Salt, Prickly Pear Teriyaki, Prickly Pear Chipotle), ~$8/bag, 3-packs $24; plus "Big Crunchy Bacony Bits" (claimed #1 meatless bacon on Amazon 2020).
- Claims on site: 600,000+ bags sold; #1 of 13 plant-protein jerkys by Tasting Table; 4.9 from 300+ verified reviews; Rowdy Girl Sanctuary donation per sale.
- Amazon: brand and products listed on amazon.com; third-party trackers/press report Amazon ratings ~3.8-4.2 vs the site's claimed 4.9+ (see limitations).
- HQ: Cross Roads, TX (Denton County); offices Frisco, Aubrey, Denton; ~6 employees (LinkedIn); founded 2018 by Brett Christoffel.

## Limitations
- Stockist locator is JS-rendered; store-level door counts by banner could not be extracted programmatically. Door counts above are company claims, not audited shelf counts.
- Amazon ratings are from third-party summaries, not a direct Amazon pull (Amazon blocks automated access). Treat as directional.
- "450+ locations" includes all banners combined; H-E-B door count is not disclosed.
