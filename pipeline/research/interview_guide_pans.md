# Interview guide: Pan's Mushroom Jerky (10–15 min, 3–5 people)

Who to interview: people who buy jerky, meat sticks, or "healthy" snacks at H-E-B at least monthly. Mix: 2 teens or college students, 1–2 parents, 1 vegetarian or flexitarian if you can.
Write down exact quotes. Save notes to `pipeline/research/interviews.md`.

1. Tell me about the last savory snack you bought. Where were you, and why that one?
2. When you walk down the jerky or snack aisle, how do you decide what to grab?
3. (Hand them a photo of a Pan's bag.) What do you think this tastes like? Who do you think it's for?
4. What would make you try this instead of your usual snack?
5. Is $8 for this bag fair? What would you compare it to?
6. If you saw a free sample of this at H-E-B, what would you do next?

**Optional taste test:** Buy 2–3 bags at H-E-B (~$25). Have each person rate it 1–5, then ask: "Would you buy it? Why or why not?" Record the scores and quotes. Real taste-test data is strong evidence for judges.
