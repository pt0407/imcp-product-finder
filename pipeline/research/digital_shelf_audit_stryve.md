# Digital shelf / distribution audit: Stryve (Braav Biltong)
Date: 2026-11-07. Method: public web audit of brand site, store locator, retailer listings, trade press.

## Findings
- Store locator (stryve.com/stores): lists national banners incl. H-E-B, Walmart, Hy-Vee, Cub Foods, Dierbergs, ACE Hardware, AAFES, HelloFresh.
- H-E-B: carries Stryve Original Sliced Beef Biltong 2.25oz in the jerky aisle, store-by-store stock (per third-party retail blog btbiltong.com, 2026).
- Walmart.com: brand storefront active; multipacks $46.99-$72.87 (e.g., 12x Original Biltong 2oz $67.18; Zero Sugar Hickory 2.25oz $72.87 = ~$520/lb equivalent).
- Sprouts: Original Biltong 2.25oz $7.99 ($3.55/oz), Thinly Sliced Steak 2oz $8.29; only ~4-8 reviews per listing.
- Category context: biltong is a nascent aisle in Texas; competitors include Brooklyn Biltong (Walmart/Costco), Shaka's, Bearded Butchers, and Texas makers Billi Tong's (SA) and Lowfeld Soul Food.
- 10-K (FY2023, via last10k): net sales $17.7M vs $29.9M prior year (~-41%); company cites discontinuation of non-profitable accounts and rationalization of low-quality revenue.
- HQ: Plano, TX. Founders: Ted Casey (Dymatize), Gabe Carimi (ex-NFL), Warren Pala (South African native).
- Products: Braav biltong line - Original, Steak Bites, Zero Sugar Hickory slices; ~$8-9 per 2.25oz bag at retail.

## Limitations
- Store-locator page is JS-rendered; per-store door counts and Texas banner counts not extractable programmatically.
- Stamped.io reviews load client-side; review corpus not fetched (documented limitation; Amazon/Walmart reviews not bulk-accessible).
- Walmart multipack prices are marketplace sellers, not necessarily shelf pricing.
