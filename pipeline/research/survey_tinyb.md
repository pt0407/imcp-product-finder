# Survey: tinyB / chocolate-gifting audience (paste into Google Forms)

**Title:** Quick gifting & dessert habits survey (2 min)
**Intro:** Hi! We're a student marketing team researching how people choose gifts and desserts. No wrong answers — honest answers help a real company. ~2 minutes.

**Q0 (Screener):** Are you between 18 and 45 years old?
- Yes / No → if No, end.

**Q1 (Screener):** In the last 3 months, did you buy chocolate or a dessert gift for someone, OR attend a workplace event with food?
- Yes / No → if No, end.

**Q2:** Which have you received or given in the last year? (select all)
- A box of chocolates for Valentine's/holiday
- A food or dessert gift from an employer
- A DIY food-making workshop (e.g., chocolate class)
- Artisan/specialty chocolate online
- None of these

**Q3:** Have you heard of "brigadeiro" or Brazilian brigadeiros before today?
- Yes / No

**Q4 (Likert 1–5):** How likely would you be to buy a $30–50 artisan chocolate gift box you discovered at a work event?

**Q5 (Likert):** How important is "gluten-free / real ingredients" when choosing a dessert gift?

**Q6:** Where do you usually discover new food or gift brands? (select up to 3)
- TikTok / Instagram / YouTube
- Friends or word of mouth
- Email newsletters
- Amazon / online search
- Retail stores
- Other: ____

**Q7:** What makes you MOST likely to order a dessert gift online? (pick one)
- A friend or colleague recommended it
- It looks unique / I can't get it in stores
- There's a discount or free shipping
- The brand's story moves me
- Reviews are excellent

**Q8 (open-ended):** What's the #1 reason you DON'T buy more artisan chocolate or dessert gifts? (one sentence)

---
Target: n ≥ 100 total, ≥ 50 passing the screener. Post in school group chats, team socials, Bay Area community groups if reachable, and family networks. Export CSV → `pipeline/research/survey_results.csv`.
