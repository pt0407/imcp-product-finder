- Channels: beestsnacks.com (DTC), Amazon, Austin & California independents; nationwide via KeHE (in progress) and Faire wholesale.
- Team: ~2 employees (LinkedIn); ~226 followers. Bootstrapped.
- Category: meat snacks +11.3% to $6.4B; high-protein snacking is the fastest-growing claim set; 'real food / non-UPF' is a rising consumer demand.
