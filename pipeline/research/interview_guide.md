# Interview guide — 10–15 min, 3–5 people in the target (Gen Z / young millennial boba + protein buyers)

Record verbatim quotes. Notes go to `pipeline/research/interviews.md`.

1. Tell me about the last time you bought boba or a protein drink. Walk me through it — where were you, what did you order, why that one?
2. How do you usually find new drink brands? (Probe: TikTok? friends? shops? Amazon?)
3. What would make you switch from your usual boba shop drink to a protein version you mix at home?
4. What feels like a fair price for a tub that makes ~20 servings? What would feel too expensive?
5. Have you tried a "protein boba" product? What did you think?
6. If you saw a protein boba brand inside a boba shop near you — like a sample cup or a poster — what would you do?
