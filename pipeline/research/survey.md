# Survey: Boba Tea Protein audience (paste into Google Forms)

**Title:** Quick drink habits survey (2 min)
**Intro text:** Hi! We're a student marketing team researching how people discover and choose drinks. No wrong answers — honest answers help a real local company. ~2 minutes.

**Q0 (Screener — required):** Are you between 16 and 34 years old?
- Yes / No → if No, end.

**Q1 (Screener):** In the last month, how often did you buy or drink boba/bubble tea OR a protein shake/smoothie?
- Never / Once / 2–3 times / Weekly / Multiple times a week → if Never, end.

**Q2:** Which of these have you bought in the last 3 months? (select all)
- Boba/bubble tea from a shop
- Protein powder to mix at home
- Ready-to-drink protein shake
- Coffee/energy drinks
- None of these

**Q3:** Have you ever heard of a "protein boba" or "boba protein" drink (a boba-flavored protein powder)?
- Yes, and I've tried one / Yes, but haven't tried / No

**Q4 (Likert 1–5, "1 = not important, 5 = very important"):** How important is protein content when you choose a snack or drink?

**Q5 (Likert):** How important is "real ingredients / no artificial stuff" to you when buying drinks?

**Q6:** Where do you usually DISCOVER new drink brands? (select up to 3)
- TikTok / Instagram / YouTube
- Friends or word of mouth
- Boba shops or cafes
- Amazon / online search
- Grocery or supplement stores
- Other: ____

**Q7:** If a $45 tub made ~20 protein boba drinks at home, how would you feel about the price?
- Way too expensive / A bit expensive / Fair price / Good deal / Amazing deal

**Q8:** Which would make you MOST likely to try a protein boba? (pick one)
- It's endorsed by a creator I follow
- A friend recommends it
- I see it in a boba shop near me
- It's on sale / first order discount
- The ingredients look clean (real tea, no sugar)

**Q9 (open-ended):** What's the #1 reason you DON'T drink more protein shakes or healthy drinks? (one sentence)

---
Target: n ≥ 100 total, ≥ 50 passing the screener. Post in school group chats, team socials, r/ApplyingToCollege-style subs, boba TikTok comment sections (follow community rules), and family networks. Export responses CSV → `pipeline/research/survey_results.csv`.
