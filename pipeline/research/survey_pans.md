# Survey: Snack habits (Pan's Mushroom Jerky) — paste into Google Forms

**Title:** 2-minute snack survey
**Intro:** We're a student marketing team studying how people pick snacks. Anonymous, ~2 minutes. Thank you!

**Q1 (Screener, required):** How old are you?
- Under 14 (end) / 14–17 / 18–24 / 25–34 / 35–54 / 55+

**Q2 (Screener):** In the past month, how often did you buy a packaged savory snack (chips, jerky, meat sticks, nuts, etc.)?
- Never (end) / Once / 2–3 times / Weekly / Several times a week

**Q3:** Where do you buy snacks most often? (pick up to 2)
- H-E-B / Walmart / Target / Costco / Convenience store or gas station / School or campus store / Online / Other

**Q4:** Which have you bought in the past 3 months? (select all)
- Beef jerky / Meat sticks (like Chomps or Slim Jim) / Plant-based meat (like Impossible or Beyond) / Mushroom or plant-based jerky / Protein bars / None of these

**Q5:** Before today, had you heard of Pan's Mushroom Jerky?
- Yes, and I've tried it / Yes, but never tried it / No

**Q6 (1–5 scale, 1 = not at all, 5 = very):** How interested are you in trying a savory, chewy jerky made from shiitake mushrooms?

**Q7:** What would make you most likely to try a new snack at the store? (pick one)
- A free sample in the store
- A coupon in my grocery app
- A friend recommends it
- A creator I follow posts about it
- It's next to snacks I already buy
- The package says what it tastes like

**Q8:** A 2.2 oz bag of mushroom jerky costs about $8. How does that price feel?
- Too cheap — I'd doubt the quality / A good value / Getting expensive / Too expensive — I wouldn't buy it

**Q9:** Which word best describes how you'd want a mushroom jerky to taste? (pick one)
- Smoky / Spicy / Sweet-savory (teriyaki) / Salty / "Just like beef" / Something new

**Q10 (open):** What is the #1 thing that would stop you from buying a plant-based snack?

---
**Goal:** 100+ total responses, 50+ who pass the screener, by Monday noon.
**Where to post:** school group chats, DECA chapter, team members' social accounts, family group chats (for the 25+ age groups).
**When done:** Google Forms → Responses → download CSV → save as `pipeline/research/survey_results.csv`.
