# Digital shelf / distribution audit: Air Plant Supply Co
Date: 2026-11-08. Method: public web audit of homepage, catalog, product page, wholesale pages, blog, dataset row.

## Findings
- Catalog: ~43 products, $6-$150 (air plants $7-15, variety packs, Spanish moss, Xerographica, terrarium kits, holiday trees, gifts under $10/$25, corporate gifts). 30-day health guarantee.
- REVIEWS: ZERO - no Judge.me/Stamped/Yotpo/Okendo/Bazaarvoice widget on homepage, /products, or a product page. No aggregateRating schema. Social proof is absent at the point of purchase.
- Mission: "$1 from every order to Pencils of Promise" appears in homepage copy; deeper story only in a 2016 blog post "x Pencils of Promise School Impact Timeline" + "Our Schools" nav (Monte Maria, Canton San Jose, Linea B-6, Santa Elena Chichop communities in Guatemala). Mission is real but effectively invisible.
- Founder: Christian - Chicago childhood -> Flagler College (St. Augustine FL) -> study abroad Costa Rica/Colombia -> began shipping air plants ~2010 (domain created Apr 2010). "Our Story" on contact page.
- Channels: DTC + wholesale (no license required, low minimums, case pricing, auto discounts $250+) + drop-shipping partnership (Shopify Collective) + corporate gifting + rooted Calathea cuttings (expanding beyond Tillandsia). Handmade air-plant holiday trees built in Guatemala.
- Traffic: ~140K monthly visits (Shopify dataset), ~43 products - real audience, thin conversion layer.
- Seasonality: heavy Q4 gifting/holiday-tree merchandising; January-February window tests year-round demand.

## Limitations
- No revenue, order volume, or conversion data public.
- No reviews anywhere on-site; Amazon presence unverified.
- Pencils of Promise agreement terms not public (brand usage rights unknown).
